Making an Ephemeral Space

I like when blog posts are as immutable as possible: a registry in time, unchanged, capturing all the mistakes, the learnings, the naiveté. New posts can almost always update what has been published before, without erasing what was previously registered. An interesting timeline is formed capturing the non-linear process of learning.

Recently I've been having the need to share with some friends the same bits of information, mostly about what tools and materials I have available in my maker space so that they can use on their own projects. I've decided, then, to create a space called "ephemerals" where I can keep these types of utilitarian information. They are not necessarily a capture of a process or a project, but a simple space that works as some sort of public clipboard I can use to send my friends. It can change to reflect the current state of things at any time or get deleted out of nowhere.

First page there is a materials library for 3D printing (i.e., the filaments I have available), but more should come soon, as I see the need for it.
